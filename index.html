IPv4 Subnet Calculator

Type an address with a prefix (192.168.10.37/26) or with a dotted mask (192.168.10.37 255.255.255.192). You get the network, broadcast, usable host range, the bits that are network and host, and a table to split the block into smaller subnets. Everything runs in your browser.
